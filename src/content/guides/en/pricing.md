**Everything that makes Breezer work is free.** Logging a pouch, your statistics, friends, the ranking, the activity feed, the global chat, and Quit Mode with its counters and savings: no subscription, no trial clock. Breezer Pro costs **€2.99 a month or €19.99 a year**, with one free week, and it buys depth rather than access.

## What you get without paying

The part most tracking apps charge for is the part that costs nothing here: the people.

| | Free |
| --- | --- |
| Snus counter | Every pouch, any brand, one tap |
| Statistics | Day, week, month and year, for the current period |
| Customised profile | Your habit, your daily limit, your brand |
| Link with friends | Add friends, see their activity |
| Ranking and activities | The full lifetime leaderboard |
| Global chat | Every channel, every message |
| Quitting | Quit Mode with days snus-free, savings and pouches avoided |
| Milestones | The first four health improvements and three symptom curves |
| Achievements | Nine of the 24 built-in ones, plus one goal you write yourself |
| Squads | Join any squad with a code |
| Drops | See every drop and every draw |
| Minigames | Three runs a day, every game, every free theme |

If all you want is to know how much you actually use, that is the whole product and you never have to look at this page again. The [snus tracker](/snus-tracker) page explains that side, and [Zyn tracker](/zyn-tracker) covers the brand question.

## What Pro adds

The app splits this the same way, because the two modes want different things.

| Full Snuser Feed | The community feed past the first six entries |
| Photos in chat | Send pictures, not just text |
| Drops & merch lotteries | Enter the draws that are marked Pro |
| Unlimited Gaming | No daily limit on minigame runs |

**Quitting**

## What Pro costs, and what it gives back

**€2.99 a month, or €19.99 a year.** One free week, once per person. The yearly plan is **€1.67 a month**, 44 percent less than twelve monthly payments, so a year costs €19.99 instead of €35.88.

Put that next to what the habit costs. A can of pouches runs about €6.50 at Austrian tobacconist prices, so **a month of Pro is less than half a can, and a whole year is about three cans**. At a can every two days, the pouches themselves cost roughly €1,190 a year. A yearly Pro subscription is under two percent of that.

Three things it buys that pay for themselves quickly: **control**, because the statistics only become useful once you can compare this month to the last one; **savings**, because Quit Mode counts what you no longer spend from your real previous use rather than an estimate; and **drops**, because the merch draws are free to watch and Pro to enter.

One honest sentence about where the money goes: Breezer currently costs more to run than it earns. Pro pays for the servers and the backend, not for profit, and that is exactly why the essential half stays free rather than being rationed.

## Where the free version ends

The generous version of this page would stop above. Here is the other half, with the real numbers, so nothing surprises you after you install.

| Limit without Pro | |
| --- | --- |
| Health improvements | 4 of 9 |
| Withdrawal symptom curves | 3 of 6 |
| Built-in achievements | 9 of 24 |
| Goals you write yourself | 1 |
| Minigame runs | 3 a day |
| Community feed | 6 entries |
| Statistics | The current period only |
| Friends ranking | Lifetime, not sliced by day, week, month or year |
| Squads | Join yes, create no |
| Drops | See yes, enter Pro-only draws no |

Two clarifications, because they are the things people assume: **the chat itself is free**, only sending photos is Pro. And **drops are not Pro as such** — every draw is visible to everyone, and only the ones flagged Pro need a subscription to enter.

## Pro without paying

Invite three friends who actually sign up and you get **30 days of full Pro**, no card, no subscription that renews. It ends by itself. That path exists because a bigger community is worth more to this app than €2.99.

## What this is all for

The goal is to build the largest snus community there is, with the useful features for every kind of person in it: whoever just wants to count, whoever is cutting down, whoever is quitting, and whoever only does any of it because friends are doing it too. Breezer is a passion project before it is a business, which is why the essentials are free and why Pro is priced at what the servers need rather than at what the market would bear.

If that sounds like something you want to be part of: the app is free, [Quit Mode](/quit-snus) is free, and the community is already there. Comparing first is fine too — [Breezer vs Snusless](/vs-snusless) and [Breezer vs Smoke Free](/vs-smoke-free) put the alternatives side by side.
