**Short answer: if you want to quit snus with friends, Breezer is the app built for that.** Smoke Free is one of the best quit-smoking apps ever made, but it was built for cigarettes and it is a solo experience. Breezer is built for snus and nicotine pouches, and the social layer is the product, not an add-on.

Both apps are free to download. Both have a quit mode. The difference is what they are for.

Smoke Free is a cigarette cessation app with a deep evidence base behind it. Breezer is a [snus app](/snus-tracker): one-tap pouch logging, friend rankings, real-time notifications when a friend takes a pouch, a global leaderboard, and a Quit Mode you switch on when you are ready, not on day one.

If your habit is snus, Zyn, Velo, LOOP or any other nicotine pouch, and you want friends involved, Breezer is the closer fit.

## Where Smoke Free genuinely wins

Smoke Free deserves its reputation. It holds a 4.8 star rating from more than 57,000 ratings on the US App Store, and the team has been building it for over a decade. The app is grounded in behaviour change science, with a large library of evidence-based techniques, a craving map that shows you geographically where your urges cluster, and a structured missions programme.

Its paid tiers go further than almost anything else in the category. You can get an AI quit coach, and at the top tier a real human stop-smoking advisor with scheduled check-ins through your first month.

If you smoke cigarettes and you want a serious, clinically-informed quit programme, Smoke Free is an excellent choice. We would recommend it over Breezer for that use case.

## Where Smoke Free falls short for snus users

The problem is not quality. It is fit.

**It is a cigarette app.** Smoke Free's store listing is written entirely around cigarettes: cigarettes not smoked, pack cost, lung recovery. Snus and nicotine pouches are not part of its stated feature set. Independent 2026 reviews of snus quit apps make the same point, noting that Smoke Free's experience remains centred on smoking even where other nicotine products are technically supported.

For a pouch user this matters in practice. Your unit is a pouch, not a cigarette. Your strength is measured in mg per pouch, not tar. Lung-recovery milestones do not describe what is happening in your body. You end up doing mental conversion maths on an app that was never modelling your habit.

**There are no friends in it.** This is the bigger gap. Smoke Free has no friend list, no rankings, no leaderboard, and no way for someone you actually know to see how you are doing. Community chat exists, but it sits behind the top paid tier and it is a chat room of strangers, not your friends.

**It assumes you have already decided to quit.** Smoke Free is built around a quit date. If you are still in the phase of wanting to see how much you actually use before committing to anything, the app has little for you.

## What Breezer does differently

### The social layer is the product

Breezer is the first social snus app. Add your friends and you get a shared, visible picture of everyone's consumption: friend rankings, a global leaderboard, an activity feed, and community chat.

The mechanic that makes it work is the real-time notification. When a friend takes a pouch, you know. That single feature turns a private habit into a visible one, and visibility is what actually changes behaviour. Nobody wants to be the one at the top of the leaderboard.

This is the thing no other snus app does. Solo trackers ask you to be accountable to a graph. Breezer makes you accountable to people who will give you grief about it.

### Track first, quit when you are ready

Breezer does not force an immediate quit date. You can use it purely as a tracker for weeks or months, watch your own numbers, and switch on [Quit Mode](/quit-snus) when you decide. When you do, Quit Mode tracks withdrawal symptoms, counts days snus-free, calculates money saved, and marks health milestones, with achievements and a quitters ranking to keep the social pressure working in your favour.

That flexibility matters because most snus users do not go from full consumption to zero in one step. They cut down first.

### Built for pouches, all of them

Breezer works with every nicotine pouch brand: [Zyn](/zyn-tracker), Velo, LOOP, and the rest. One tap logs a pouch. The statistics show daily, weekly and monthly consumption, spend, and habit patterns in charts built for pouch counts.

### Made by a snuser

Breezer was built in Lower Austria by a snus user, for snus users, and it is developed with feedback from the community using it. It currently holds a 4.8 star average across 27 reviews on the App Store and Google Play, with users in Austria and Poland.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Smoke Free if:** you smoke cigarettes, you have already committed to a quit date, and you want a clinically-informed programme with professional coaching available.

**Choose Breezer if:** your habit is snus or nicotine pouches, you want friends involved, you want to see your real consumption before committing to quitting, or you want the option to compete rather than just abstain.

They are not really competing for the same person. Smoke Free is the best-known quit-smoking app. Breezer is the social snus app.
