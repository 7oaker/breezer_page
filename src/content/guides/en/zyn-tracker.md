Zyn, Velo, LOOP, and other nicotine pouches are everywhere, but most users have no idea how many they actually use. Breezer works as a Zyn tracker for all brands: log pouches, set limits, see costs, and compete with friends.

## Why use a Zyn tracker?
Nicotine pouches feel effortless, which is exactly why consumption creeps up unnoticed. A Zyn tracker makes the real numbers visible: how many pouches a day, what it costs, and how your nicotine intake stacks up week to week.

Breezer logs each pouch with a single tap, shows your trends in charts, and reminds you when you hit your daily limit. Stay in control without having to quit right away.

## All brands, one tracker
Unlike brand-specific tools, Breezer works with every nicotine pouch: Zyn, Velo, LOOP, XQS, Killa, Pablo, and more. You can track multiple brands at the same time and see your total consumption at a glance.

The tracker is part of the broader [Breezer Snus App](/) with social rankings, friends, and a quit mode, all in one place.

## Zyn tracker features
- One-tap logging for every nicotine pouch
- Brand- and strength-agnostic tracking
- Daily, weekly, and monthly consumption stats
- Cost tracking per pouch and per month
- Limit reminders when you hit your daily target
- Lock screen widget for quick logging
- Global and local social rankings

## From tracking to quitting
Many Zyn users start with tracking and gradually move toward reducing. Breezer supports both. When you're ready, switch to [Quit Mode](/quit-snus) to track withdrawal symptoms, health milestones, and savings, without changing apps.
